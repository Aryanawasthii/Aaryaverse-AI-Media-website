The Person Behind the Brand

My Journey. My Learning. My Vision.

I’m Aryan Awasthi, Founder of Aaryaverse AI Media.

My professional journey has been shaped by leadership, communication, digital media, sales and business operations.

I began my professional journey with an EdTech internship, where I developed an understanding of lead management, customer communication and follow-ups.

I then moved to Media Sathi, where I gained practical exposure to social media management, content and digital marketing activities. This experience strengthened my interest in the digital media industry.

Later, at LeadsWallah, I took on broader operational responsibilities, working across recruitment, team management, sales training, client communication and business operations. I also developed practical experience in pitching, objection handling and coordinating teams.

Alongside my professional journey, my experience in NSS leadership helped me develop teamwork, responsibility and people-management skills. I had the opportunity to lead more than 100 volunteers and received recognition for my contribution.

With a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) background and an interest in technology, creativity and business growth, I decided to take the next step towards entrepreneurship.

That decision led to the beginning of Aaryaverse AI Media.

Today, my vision is to build a digital marketing agency that combines creative thinking, strategic marketing and modern technology to help businesses grow.

I’m not here to claim that I know everything. I’m here to build, learn, experiment and create something meaningful.

Aaryaverse AI Media is not just a business idea. It’s the beginning of a vision I’m determined to build.

— Aryan Awasthi
